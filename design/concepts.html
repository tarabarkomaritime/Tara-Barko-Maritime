Brand foundation

Shared by all five concepts

Every concept draws from the same palette, the same mark and the same two-tab structure. What changes between them is proportion, density and tone of voice — not the brand.

Primary mark — full lockup

Typography

System sans throughout. No web-font download, so the portal stays fast on a mobile connection at the pier.

Train with Tara Barko.
Section heading — 19px / 800
Body copy runs 13–14.5px on screen with a 1.45 line height. Field labels are 12px semibold; document numbers use a monospace face so APP-2026-0006 stays legible.

Colour discipline

The two brand colours do different jobs. Mixing those jobs is what makes a two-colour brand look cheap.

RoleColourApplies to
StructureBlueHeaders, headings, navigation, data, fee figures, timelines
ActionOrangeThe primary button on any screen, the active Enroll tab, required-field markers, the reference code
Warning onlyOrangeLow seat counts — never as decoration, so it keeps its urgency
Never—Orange body text, orange backgrounds behind long copy, two orange buttons on one screen

Information architecture — identical in all five

TabContains
CoursesHero, open schedules with live seat counts, searchable course catalogue
Enroll NowNew Enrollment — 3-step wizard (schedule → details → review)
Track Enrollment — reference code + last name, status timeline

Track Enrollment sits inside Enroll Now rather than in the top bar: an applicant looking for it has already enrolled, and looks where they enrolled. Staff sign-in lives in the footer — it is not a public destination.

Numbered sections, underline fields, no decoration. The whole form fits one desktop screen.

Where it wins

Speed and legibility. Numbered sections match how the registrar reads a file, and the underline treatment survives printing — useful when an agency wants a paper copy for twelve seafarers.

What it costs

No warmth and no persuasion. A first-time visitor who has not decided to enroll yet gets nothing here to help them decide.

Comparison & recommendation

Choose one, or one plus a borrow
ConceptFeels like Strongest atWeakest atBuild
01 Harbour DeepAn institution Trust at the moment you ask for SRN, passport and next of kin Mobile — the dark hero eats the first screenLow
02 BeaconA campaign Turning a Facebook click into a submitted application Credibility; a lot of orange for a regulated serviceLow
03 Sail SplitA modern brand Being remembered, and using the mark structurally Responsive cost — the diagonal must be rebuilt under 820pxHigh
04 ManifestAn official form Speed for repeat users and agencies; printing Persuading anyone who has not already decidedLow
05 CoastalA consumer app Mobile completion rate across thirteen fields Reading as an accredited training centerMedium
Recommendation — 01 Harbour Deep for the shell, 04 Manifest for the form. The two screens are doing different jobs and deserve different treatments. The Courses tab has to earn trust and sell a seat, which is 01's strength. The Enroll Now form has to be filled in accurately by someone on a phone who has already decided — which is 04's. They share the palette and the mark, so combining them is a change of density, not of brand.

What to borrow regardless of the choice

FromBorrow
02 BeaconThe three-number stat strip. It answers "is there a seat for me?" before the visitor has to search for one.
03 Sail SplitThe review layout — details left, money right, with the fee disclaimer directly under the figure it qualifies.
04 ManifestNumbered form sections. A seafarer on the phone to the registrar can say "I'm stuck on section 4".
05 CoastalThe phone layout of the acknowledgement screen. The reference code is the single most important thing the portal ever shows.

Constant across all five — do not trade these away

These come from how the system actually works, not from taste.

Two tabs onlyCourses and Enroll Now. Track Enrollment is a sub-tab inside Enroll Now.
Live seat countsShown net of applications awaiting approval, so the portal never oversells a batch.
Orange means actOne orange button per screen. Orange elsewhere only for required markers and low seat warnings.
The fee is an estimateStated next to the figure, every time. No payment is collected on the website.
The reference code is the heroLargest element on the acknowledgement screen, and printable.
Emergency contact is requiredBefore any practical or sea-survival exercise. The form says why.
