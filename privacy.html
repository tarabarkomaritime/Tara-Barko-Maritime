Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Tara Barko Maritime Services ("Tara Barko", "we", "us"), including TB - Maritime Training and Assessment Endorsement, helps seafarers enroll in MARINA and STCW training through our accredited partner training centers. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers our website, our enrollment forms, and messages you send to our Facebook pages and Messenger.

We handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

1. Information we collect

  • From Messenger and our Facebook pages: your Facebook name, the page-scoped ID that Facebook gives us, the messages and comments you send, and any images or files you send (for example, payment screenshots or a photo of your ID).
  • For enrollment: your full name, contact details, the course and schedule you choose, and the documents the training requires (such as a valid ID, SRN, COP, Seaman's Book details and medical certificate).
  • For payment: the screenshot or confirmation of your payment. We do not collect or store your card or bank login details.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions about courses, fees, requirements and schedules, including through an automated assistant on our Facebook pages.
  • To process your enrollment and endorse you to the partner training center that will conduct your training.
  • To verify your payment and send you your receipt and training instructions.
  • To follow up on your inquiry and to let our staff take over your conversation when needed.
  • To keep records required for accounting and regulatory purposes.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising outside our own pages.

3. Automated replies on Messenger

Some replies on our Facebook pages are sent automatically, based on fixed answers about our courses, fees and requirements. Our staff review conversations and step in for payments, documents, rescheduling, cancellations, refunds and anything the automated replies cannot answer. Images you send are passed to our staff and are not interpreted automatically.

4. Who we share it with

  • Partner training centers: the details needed to enroll and train you.
  • MARINA and other government agencies: where needed for your certificates, COP or Seaman's Book processing, or where the law requires it.
  • Service providers: companies that host our systems and data (such as our database and email providers) and Meta Platforms, which operates Facebook and Messenger. They process data only to provide their services to us.

5. How we keep it safe and how long we keep it

Your information is stored in secured, access-controlled systems that only authorized staff can use. We keep Messenger conversation records for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and enrollment, and enrollment and payment records for as long as required for accounting and regulatory purposes. After that, we delete or anonymize them.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to its processing, or withdraw your consent. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

7. How to request deletion of your data

To delete the information we hold about you, including your Messenger conversation records with our pages:

  1. Email tarabarkomaritime@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", or send the same request to us on Messenger.
  2. Include your full name and the Facebook name you used to message us.

We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days. Records we are required by law to keep (such as official receipts) will be kept only for the required period.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
